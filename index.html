Four bedrooms, three full bathrooms and open living spaces. The revised design features a vaulted front bedroom, a canted bay glazed to the floor, two foyer skylights, a skylight over the primary shower, a sliding door from the primary bedroom to the garden, a high entry window, white window frames with grid bars throughout, a living room with the sofa on the east wall facing the television, a warm vintage kitchen in cream, oak and brass, a white primary bathroom with one wall of stacked stone, a wood-look feature wall in the shared bathroom and a cedar carriage-house garage door.
